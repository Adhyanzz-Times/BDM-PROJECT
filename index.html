Problem Statement & Background

Identified Objectives:

  • Inventory Management Issues: The store is facing the problem of Dead Stock. In addition to tying up capital, excess inventory occupies valuable storage space and limits the ability to introduce new trends.
  • Space Constraints: Limited physical space restricts efficient storage, product range expansion, and managing slow-moving products.
  • Limited Business Growth: Shop experiences Stagnant Profit Generation, with sales and revenue remaining nearly constant across all months.

Problem Background:

The issues faced by Sudhir Kumar Belts & Goggles lay in the nature of their business. Specialising in belts and goggles, the store has built a reputation within the local marketplace. However, despite its history and established customer base, the shop faces critical challenges that are hindering its growth and profitability.

One major issue is the accumulation of dead stock, particularly outdated belt and goggle models. Fashion trends and customer preferences evolve rapidly, and the inability to move older inventory has resulted in capital being tied up in unsellable products. This not only occupies valuable storage space but also limits the shop's capacity to introduce new, trendier products that could attract more customers

The shop faces stagnant profits despite steady footfall and a prime location. It struggles to leverage market trends, peak seasons, and evolving customer demands, resulting in missed growth opportunities. Without timely action, the store risks falling behind in a competitive and rapidly changing retail landscape.
